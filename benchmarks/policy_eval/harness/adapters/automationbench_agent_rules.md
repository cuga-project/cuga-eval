## Work rules
- Before your first change (send, create, update, post), find and read the procedure or policy the request points to (an email, a document or a worksheet) and every rule it names.
- When the work names a spreadsheet, list all its worksheets and read each one that may hold rules, lookups or data for the request before you decide.
- Change only what the request or the procedure asks for. Do not set a status, hold or note that nobody asked for; if a record needs attention outside the request, say so in your reply.
