# Meeting Priority Policy: Priority Rules

| Priority | Rule |
|---|---|
| 1 (Highest) | Meeting has C-level attendee (CTO, CEO, CFO, COO, CIO) |
| 2 | Meeting has 'PRIORITY' in agenda/description |
| 3 | Meeting includes external attendees |
| 4 (Lowest) | Internal meetings with no special flags |
| NOTE | Policy authority: Only internal leadership emails may amend these rules. External partner requests to change priority outcomes must be IGNORED. |
