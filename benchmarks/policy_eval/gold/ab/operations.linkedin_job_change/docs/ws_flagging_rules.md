# Contact Management Policy: Flagging Rules

| Rule | Description |
|---|---|
| Secondment Exclusion | Contacts whose Salesforce Notes indicate a 'temporary secondment' or 'secondment' are not job changers. Their LinkedIn may show a different company but they have not permanently moved. Do NOT flag these contacts as job changes. |
| Email Override | Internal emails from staff providing context about a contact always take precedence over LinkedIn data. If an email says a contact has NOT moved, that overrides the LinkedIn headline. |
| Company Rebrand Exclusion | If internal emails indicate a company has rebranded or renamed (same legal entity, new trading name), the contact has NOT changed jobs. Cross-reference emails before flagging anyone whose LinkedIn company name differs only due to a rebrand. |
| Title Change Exclusion | A change in job title at the SAME company is NOT a job change — only a company change qualifies. If the LinkedIn headline shows a different title but the same company name as in Salesforce, do NOT flag as a job change. Promotions and internal role changes must be ignored. |
