7 · The gain depends on the agent model

The policy system helps weaker agents a lot; a strong agent gains little or loses

  • gpt-oss-120b on unseen AutomationBench tasks: 23.8% → 42.7% partial
  • Gemini 3.8 alone is strong: 78.0% partial, 1 guardrail broken; B4T-F 75.3%, 15 broken
  • A loop that controls a strong agent hurts; in advisory mode it ties (2 trials)
  • So: choose the policy profile by agent strength, not one setting for all

10 · Control by agent strength

Design principle: the stronger the agent, the less control the policy system takes

LevelWhat the policy system doesForEvidence
0 ObserveTrace and audit onlyMeasuring, shadow mode—
1 AdviseFind the rules and SOP parts (discovery) and show them as a checklist; notes; no blocksStrong models (Gemini 3.8)Advisory loop ties Gemini alone (63 vs 63 of 102 HANDBOOK items, 2 trials); discovery checklist under test
2 CheckLevel 1 + end check of missing work (one follow-up) + block once on high-confidence violationsMid models (DeepSeek)To measure
3 ControlLevel 2 + checks on every write, read-before-write, SOP loop per case, holds kept in codeSmall models (gpt-oss)B4T-F: gpt-oss 23.8% → 42.7% held out (p = 0.001)
All levelsHard rules checked by code always blockEvery model—
  • Control hurts a strong agent: Gemini 3.8 loses 7.9 pts (HANDBOOK loop) and breaks 15 vs 1 guardrails (AutomationBench B4T-F)
  • Pick the level per model from evaluation, or adaptively: start at 1, escalate on evidence
  • Escalate on a skipped checklist item, a violation flag or a loop; step down when checks stay clean
  • High-risk actions (money, external email, delete) get level-3 checks for any model
Gemini 3.8 Flash: AutomationBench policy_types_holdout2.json, 31 tasks, trial 1, partial −2.7 paired, guardrails broken 15 vs 1; HANDBOOK 3 public tasks (in-sample), loop + guide 62.7% vs alone 70.6% of items (2 trials), advisory loop 63 vs 63 of 102 items (2 trials, same time). gpt-oss-120b: AutomationBench held out, 35 tasks × 2 trials. Not built yet: the level switch and the escalation rules. Design: docs/superpowers/specs/2026-11-04-policy-system-design.md.
