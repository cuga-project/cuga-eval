Policies enter before or during the run; checks run at CUGA's hooks

  • Online intake: code filter, then Kev-4B, then a small LLM.
  • A write waits for pending intake, at most 45 s.
  • Outcome: allow, deny with a remedy, or remind.
  • CUGA plugs in the layer only through its hooks API.
