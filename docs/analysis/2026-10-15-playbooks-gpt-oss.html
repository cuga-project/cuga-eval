What we did

We turned a strong model's mistakes into playbooks for a smaller model

  • The playbooks are the only difference between the two runs
  • Same tasks, same agent version, same time limits
The playbooks

A playbook is a short how-to for one type of task

Changing many itemsShoppingSplitting moneyCounting questionsUsing notesFiles and emailFollowing a message
  • Email "from or to" a person: they sent it, or you did
  • Before ordering, remove other items from the cart
  • General rules only: no task names, no answers
  • Written with CUGA's playbook skill, in CUGA's own format
For each task, CUGA picks the one playbook that best matches it.
