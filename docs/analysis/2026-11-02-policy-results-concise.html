Policy layer for CUGA: what we learned (2026-11-02)

Four benchmarks, held-out tests. Agent: DeepSeek-V4-Pro (function calling). Policy layer: gpt-oss-120b.

Benchmarks: AutomationBench, ITSM (EnterpriseOps-Gym), HANDBOOK, τ2 airline. Source: docs/analysis/2026-11-02-overnight-log.md.

The policy layer did not improve held-out scores.

  • unseen3: the 95% interval includes 0, so the gain is not proven.
  • The configuration was frozen before each held-out run.
10 tasks per set; noise about ±6 pts per trial. AutomationBench: partial; ITSM: verifiers. Source: overnight log (02:40, 14:40).

There are few policy violations to catch.

  • AutomationBench: the other failed checks are missing or wrong work.
  • ITSM: the agent already follows the checkable rules.
  • All are below our 30% bar for running a gate.
AutomationBench: failed checks of CUGA alone on unseen2 (30 runs). τ2 airline: CUGA alone, first 20 tasks, 1 trial. ITSM: policy layer actions with the DeepSeek-V4-Pro agent. Source: history.md (2026-11-02), overnight log (01:32, 19:30, 21:00).

A checklist of required effects matches what graders check.

  • AutomationBench, ITSM: failed checks. HANDBOOK: all rubric items.
  • HANDBOOK coverage needs the data the agent read.
Offline replay against the grader checks; no agent run. HANDBOOK compiler: DeepSeek-V4-Pro. Source: overnight log (15:30, 16:40).

But tracker feedback did not help: wrong feedback costs more than right feedback gains.

  • The gate wrongly flags 17–40% of checks that passed.
  • Follow-ups on inferred values made the agent change correct records (ITSM −13.2 pts, first round).
17–40%: ITSM gate replay, two gate versions. Source: overnight log (16:40, 18:00, 21:00).

When told exactly what failed, the agent fixes most of it.

  • AutomationBench: +35.9 pts (95% CI +15.8 to +58.2); tasks passed 2.0 → 6.0 of 10.
  • ITSM: +25.3 pts; tasks passed 9 → 18 of 20.
  • One feedback round cut failed checks from 93 to 24 (AutomationBench).
Oracle: the grader's failed checks, with expected values, sent back after the answer (at most 2 rounds). An upper bound, not a method. Source: overnight log (2026-11-02, oracle).
